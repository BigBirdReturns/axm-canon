Current retained review surface:

- 726 model-recall candidates;
- 79 conversation-synthesis candidates;
- 805 combined candidates in 15 packets;
- 219 registered source identities;
- 45 research lanes;
- exact-edition intake for plain text, HTML, and EPUB;
- deterministic private retrieval;
- source-specific rights and authority controls;
- reviewed `confirm`, `correct`, `split`, `merge`, `reject`, and `defer` transactions.

Those counts describe a pre-ingestion review estate. They do not mean the five novels have been ingested, and the 805 candidates are not canon. The next product transaction is an exact holder-controlled AGOT edition, chapter and paragraph locators, reviewed claims, and the first readable source-backed lore atlas.

Tier Bench may execute bounded work against this estate. It is not part of the estate and cannot acquire canon authority from scheduling, transport, process, or operating-system receipts.
