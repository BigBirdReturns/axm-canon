# ASOIAF public source descent registry v1

This component is a repository-native public metadata derivative of the missing sealed `asoiaf-public-source-descent-v0.1.tar.zst` object. It records twelve source identities, ten transmission or custody relations, thirteen exact-witness gaps, nine routes applicable to released-future material, six routes applicable to So Spake Martin descent, and one archival-access service.

The twelve records are source and transmission classes, not source text. George R. R. Martin remains the author and process speaker; publishers and app operators hold edition or platform custody; convention organizers establish event context; broadcasters and rights holders govern recordings; named attendees, reporters, and submitters remain distinct from archive operators; Westeros.org curates public SSM and news routes; A Wiki of Ice and Fire supplies secondary navigation; and the Internet Archive supplies capture access only. `axm-canon` acts as package custodian and acquires no source authority from admission.

The registry contains no chapter prose, transcript, attendee-notes text, publisher edition bytes, app package, media file, archive capture, or holder-controlled payload. A live route does not prove a historical version, a Wayback service URL does not prove a capture, an attendee report does not become author text, and a secondary inventory does not become a primary witness. Automatic canon promotions and graph mutations remain zero.

The sealed predecessor remains identified at 22,011 bytes and SHA-256 `db3a3c0073a742cf4bd6137e40fd3f7a403a74f415c015aecf68c1f0d052ed2e`. Matching its 12/10/13/9/6/1 census establishes integration-shape compatibility only. It does not reproduce the predecessor bytes or prove record equivalence.

The control question is whether every public source identity can be replaced by a timestamped, versioned, rights-bounded witness without allowing route availability, archive access, reporter mediation, dependency custody, or count compatibility to impersonate source text, publication identity, canon authority, or graph authority.
