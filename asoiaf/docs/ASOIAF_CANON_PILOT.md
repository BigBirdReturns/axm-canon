# ASOIAF Canon Pilot

## Purpose

The pilot will compile the *A Song of Ice and Fire* source estate into a deterministic constraint bible capable of evaluating adaptation decisions, reconstructing bounded background history, and exposing the remaining closure lattice around the unpublished endgame. It will not present generated continuation as George R. R. Martin's text or intent. Its authority is limited to source-backed facts, explicit assumptions, world-law constraints, and deterministic consequences of selected branch decisions.

The source estate is unusually suitable for this method because its possibility space is simultaneously large and bounded. The books leave enormous room below the principal character line, including households, retainers, roads, ports, harvests, marriages, rumors, refugees, minor houses, religious networks, trade, and local violence. The major chronology, geography, dynastic claims, planted mechanisms, and several terminal coordinates sharply constrain how those local possibilities can connect to the endgame.

## Corpus planes

The corpus must remain partitioned by authority and continuity.

### Primary novel continuity

The five published main novels form the controlling narrative record. Each user-supplied edition receives an exact digest and locator map. Chapter identity, viewpoint, internal chronology, actor presence, actor knowledge, location, explicit claim, reported claim, dream, prophecy, rumor, and material state must remain distinguishable.

### Published companion history

The Dunk and Egg novellas, *Fire & Blood*, *The World of Ice & Fire*, published Targaryen histories, maps, appendices, and other authorized companion material enter as separately identified sources. Their historical narrators and editorial framing remain part of the evidence record. A claim reported by a maester is not silently upgraded into omniscient fact.

### Released future-book material

Officially released sample chapters and readings enter as versioned sources with date and venue. Superseded readings remain in custody rather than being overwritten, because revision history can reveal which mechanisms Martin retained, changed, or abandoned.

### Author statements

Martin's public statements about intended endpoints, writing process, character survival, adaptation differences, and source inspirations enter as `author-statement` sources. Each statement needs exact date, venue, wording locator, and scope. A statement about a television endpoint is not automatically a complete description of the book mechanism.

### Adaptation and production testimony

HBO scripts, episodes, official extras, interviews with David Benioff, D. B. Weiss, Ryan Condal, actors, directors, and production staff enter as separate adaptation and production-testimony continuities. They are evidence about choices, disclosed endpoint coordinates, and implementation history. They do not overwrite book canon.

### Historical, religious, and literary analogues

The Wars of the Roses, medieval dynastic practice, steppe and maritime logistics, Norse and Celtic material, alchemy, ritual sacrifice, Christian and pre-Christian religious structures, Tolkien, historical chronicles, and other demonstrated source influences enter as `historical-analogue` or `analysis`. They may generate hypotheses or constrain plausible mechanics only where the corpus establishes the relevant borrowing or where ordinary physical history is being used as a bounded model. Resemblance alone does not create canon.

## Initial compilation shards

The first useful estate should be built as interoperable shards rather than one unreviewable monolith.

### Chronology and geography

This shard records every bounded event window, named route, travel duration, seasonal condition, raven or ship channel, army movement, and character location. It should distinguish explicit dates, inferred ordering, and disputed synchronization. The first refusal tests are teleportation, impossible message latency, and armies or fleets appearing without a viable route.

### Lineage, marriage, office, and claim

This shard records biological claims, legal parentage, acknowledged bastardy, marriages, betrothals, inheritance customs, offices, oaths, hostage relationships, wards, and competing theories of legitimacy. Contradictory parentage or identity claims remain proposition variants with attestation receipts. Succession outcomes are compiled through the selected legal and political assumptions rather than one invented universal law.

### Actor knowledge and belief

This shard records what each actor knows, believes, suspects, rejects, or has no available channel to learn at each bounded point. It prevents a later scene from using reader knowledge as character knowledge and exposes which raven, witness, dream, glass candle, greenseer, confession, rumor network, or captured document must carry new information.

### Material and institutional state

This shard records food, treasury, military strength, fleet custody, castles, roads, ports, harvests, population pressure, hostages, dragon assets, religious authority, and administrative control at the appropriate resolution. Major actors and smallfolk share the same state rather than occupying separate narrative universes. A siege, migration, riot, epidemic, or policy choice propagates into households, markets, recruitment, disease, legitimacy, and local violence.

### Magic and world physics

This shard records observed capabilities, costs, transfer media, conditions, failure modes, and epistemic limits for dragons, warging, greenseeing, resurrection, blood magic, fire visions, glass candles, glamours, the Others, the weirwood network, Valyrian artifacts, seasons, and other nonordinary mechanisms. The initial rule is conservative: observed effects and supported constraints are admitted; cosmological explanations remain propositions unless the corpus establishes them.

A sacrifice may be recorded as a transaction with a victim, transferred substance, medium, invocation, observed response, and unresolved counterparty. The engine may then constrain what kind of event happened without inventing the god's identity.

### Narrative-function registry

This shard records the work performed by every major and secondary storyline. Candidate functions include rival claimant, popular restoration, bell trauma, hidden wildfire, northern legitimacy, greenseer memory, magical warning, fleet delivery, religious mobilization, dragon access, hostage leverage, and postwar settlement. Deleting or merging a television character must transfer, transform, or explicitly retire each inherited function.

The first adaptation audit should compile the removal of Young Griff, Jon Connington, Arianne Martell, Victarion, Lady Stoneheart, the deeper Euron machinery, and other major divergences. The target is not to prove one fan theory. It is to identify which source functions were lost and whether the selected television substitutions were causally equivalent.

### Endgame coordinate ledger

Known and attributed endpoints enter with evidence tier, temporal window, and mechanism status. Examples may include Martin-attributed destinations reported through production, book-planted terminal pressures, and television-only resolutions. Each endpoint must distinguish:

- destination known;
- mechanism partly known;
- mechanism unknown;
- adaptation-only implementation;
- disputed attribution.

The closure compiler should therefore be able to hold “Bran enters sovereign authority” as a terminal coordinate while rejecting the assumption that the television council scene is the required mechanism.

## Background-lore generation

Background generation begins only after the relevant shards compile. The unit is a proposed event or state transition, not a paragraph of prose. A candidate minor-house defection, village evacuation, marriage compact, market failure, religious procession, bandit migration, ship loss, or household rumor must declare:

- bounded time and location;
- actors and institutions involved;
- information available to them;
- material resources and costs;
- causal parents;
- proposition changes;
- downstream obligations;
- source or rule receipts;
- continuity and branch identity.

The compiler then classifies it as necessary, strongly implied, permissible, or contradicted. Accepted background history can be rendered into prose later, but the prose remains a presentation over the accepted event receipt. Repeated compilation of the same corpus, branch, and proposal set must produce the same state and identity.

This is how the estate can travel orders of magnitude away from the hero line without losing the major line. A local harvest failure can generate price changes, migration, disease, recruitment, banditry, lordly coercion, religious response, and altered message or army movement. Those events remain bounded by known geography, season, institutions, resources, and terminal chronology. The local world can be vast while the global resolution remains constrained.

## First ingestion transaction

The first user-supplied book should be processed as a custody transaction rather than copied into the repository.

1. Record title, edition, format, file size, and cryptographic digest.
2. Produce a byte-exact or page-exact locator index in holder-controlled storage.
3. Extract chapter boundaries, viewpoint identity, named entities, explicit dates, locations, and direct relation claims into a review queue.
4. Bind every accepted normalized record to one or more exact locators.
5. Compile a Genesis strict-evidence shard.
6. Mount the shard in Spectra for query and correction.
7. Publish only nonpayload schemas, digests, derived records, and qualification receipts to the public branch.

No automatic extractor output receives authoritative standing until a human-reviewed record has exact source custody and passes the canon validator.

## Pilot order

The recommended first source is the opening novel because it establishes the base entity, chronology, geography, household, office, lineage, and knowledge vocabulary. The second pass should ingest appendices and maps, then continue through the main novels in publication order. Companion histories and released sample chapters should be added as separate source strata after the base novel continuity can already compile.

The adaptation audit can begin before the full corpus is complete by using a bounded function slice. A useful first case is the Young Griff decision because it exercises identity uncertainty, rival claim, popular legitimacy, Varys's project, Jon Connington's trauma, Golden Company custody, bells, and Daenerys's arrival position. The compiler should accept removal only when the decision set names explicit substitutes for the required functions or produces a refusal receipt showing the orphaned work.

## Acceptance boundary

The first ASOIAF pilot is accepted when it can:

- ingest one exact user-held edition without publishing its text;
- recover every accepted claim to bounded source locators;
- preserve contradictory testimony;
- compile at least two explicit continuity branches;
- reject one impossible chronology or travel proposal;
- reject one action based on unavailable knowledge;
- reject one material or magical cost violation;
- propagate one character deletion into a required function reassignment;
- enumerate at least two valid routes to one fixed endpoint;
- generate one smallfolk-scale event chain whose receipts remain connected to the major chronology;
- reproduce byte-identical branch and proposal receipts across repeated runs.

The control question is whether the system can widen the world indefinitely at local scale while making every route back into the major chronology prove its source, mechanism, cost, and inherited consequence.
