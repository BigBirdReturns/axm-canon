## What the discussion contributes

The Dance packet records the family structure that made the show confusing,
the legal and biological layers around Rhaenyra's sons, the accumulated
dragonseed population, the Cargyll twins' institutional function, Larys's
under-specified collection network, Daemon's command responsibility for the
Blackwood terror campaign, Willem Blackwood's later use as a political receipt,
Jace's dragon-monopoly concern, and the distinction between dragon bonding and
constitutional legitimacy.

The religion packet records that Varys's castration, burning, chant, altered
flame, and answering voice form one ritual transaction. It keeps R'hllor as the
leading institutional attribution while preserving an unnamed fire intelligence
as a live alternative. It also records the oracle, dedication, and continuing
contract branches; reproductive future as concentrated sacrificial value;
fire as transfer medium; Kinvara's special knowledge; the Cybele, Attis, and
Galli analogue; and the darker reading in which dragonfire completes what the
childhood fire began.

The endpoint packet records the fixed resolution of the Dance, the Hour of the
Wolf, the distinction between Green historical numbering and Black dynastic
survival, the destruction of the dragon monopoly, and the requirement that a
known-history adaptation generate suspense from mechanism rather than winner
uncertainty. It also carries the production-bible consequences of deleting
Young Griff, underbuilding Bran's accession, and compressing Daenerys's causal
collapse.

The endgame packet records Martin-attributed coordinates separately from
show-only mechanisms. Bran's sovereignty, Hodor's time loop, Shireen's burning,
the bittersweet Scouring-like aftermath, Young Griff's identity variants,
Connington's bells, King's Landing wildfire, Daenerys arriving against a prior
restoration, book Euron's magical escalation, the Others' metaphysical burden,
and Jon's possible northern endpoint remain distinct review objects.

The method packet records the rules the discussion discovered: narrative
functions survive character deletion; actor knowledge remains separate from
world truth; contradictory narrators remain contradictory; local history may
expand far below the hero line; every expansion pays travel, information,
material, institutional, and magical costs; historical analogues constrain
without becoming canon; community consensus remains a separate authority tier;
and fixed endpoints compile into a closure lattice rather than one counterfeit
unwritten novel.
